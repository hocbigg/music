## Compose your own instrumental piece

- [How to Compose Music 101 (Art of Composing)](https://www.artofcomposing.com/how-to-compose-music-101) - A step-by-step walkthrough detailing how to construct basic motifs, extend them into functional phrases, and structure a finished instrumental piece.
- [Musical Composition: Craft and Art (Alan Belkin)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSntcNF64SVVhPiEzHVIjIP0TvutZhK8J) - A comprehensive 54-video masterclass walking through motif development, formal punctuation, transitions, and ending strategies for original works.

## Write your own song

- [How to Write a Song: Lyrics, Melody, Structure, Hooks (Orphiq)](https://orphiq.com/resources/how-to-write-a-song-guide) - A practical guide covering ideation, prosody, lyric sketching, verse-chorus architecture, and final harmonic arrangement.
- [How to Write a Song — Start-to-Finish (Andrea Stolpe)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBknUU6YCPM) - A real-time video walkthrough turning an initial sensory free-writing exercise into verses, pre-choruses, choruses, and an arranged acoustic demo.

## Produce your own recorded track

- [Beginner Music Production: Your Step-by-Step Guide (SoundBridge)](https://soundbridge.io/en/beginner-music-production-your-step-by-step-guide) - A five-stage production walkthrough detailing DAW setup, MIDI tracking, audio editing, rough balancing, and final bounce.
- [The Music Production Workflow (DIY Music Guide)](https://www.diy-music-guide.com/blog/the-music-production-workflow) - A structured framework for taking music systematically through the sketching, demoing, multitracking, and final export stages.
- [Creative Strategies for Electronic Music Producers (Dennis DeSantis / Ableton)](https://makingmusic.ableton.com) - A concrete problem-solving manual offering concrete workflows for overcoming the loop trap, developing arrangements, and completing finished tracks.

## Create your own track remix

- [How to Make a Great Remix: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners (Beatportal)](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/667591-how-to-make-a-great-remix-a-step-by-step-guide-for-beginners) - A production walkthrough on acquiring stems, tempo-matching acapellas, choosing harmonic directions, and restructuring a track for club or streaming release.

## Transcribe your own lead sheet

- [How to Transcribe Music: The Ultimate Guide (Composer Code)](https://composercode.com/ultimate-transcription-guide/) - A step-by-step case study demonstrating how to isolate basslines, identify time signatures, transcribe vocal or instrumental melodies, and label chords into a standard lead sheet.
- [How to Transcribe Music by Ear (Music: Practice & Theory Stack Exchange)](https://music.stackexchange.com/questions/46/how-to-transcribe-music-by-ear) - A practitioner-vetted workflow guide breaking down tonic identification, interval mapping, harmonic cadence recognition, and rhythmic notation.

## Write your own musical analysis

- [How to Analyze Music Using the 5-Point Technique (EDMProd)](https://www.edmprod.com/analyze-music) - A structural framework and worksheet for breaking down commercial recordings across composition, arrangement, sound design, mix balance, and master processing.
- [Writing About Music (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/music/) - An academic methodology guide on translating musical elements, harmonic shifts, and stylistic nuances into a coherent analytical argument.
- [How to Analyse Music — Part 1 (Practising the Piano)](https://practisingthepiano.com/how-to-analyse-music-part-1) - A practical walkthrough showing performers how to dissect formal architecture, harmonic cadence points, and thematic motifs to understand how a score functions.

## Design your own synthesizer patch

- [Learning Synths (Ableton)](https://learningsynths.ableton.com) - An interactive browser playground providing hands-on tutorials for synthesizing bass, lead, pad, and percussion sounds from raw oscillators and envelopes.
- [Synth Secrets (Gordon Reid / Sound on Sound)](https://www.soundonsound.com/series/synth-secrets-sound-sound) - A 63-part pedagogical series providing precise acoustic recipes and filter/envelope routings to recreate authentic acoustic and electronic instruments from scratch.
