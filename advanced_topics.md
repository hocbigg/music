# Music: Advanced Topics

This page assumes you have completed the Core Undergraduate Curriculum in Music and are ready to specialize in advanced theoretical craft, contemporary genres, or studio technologies. The five specialization tracks below represent the major applied and analytical disciplines in contemporary music. Learners are encouraged to select one or two tracks aligned with their specific musical ambitions rather than attempting to study all five sequentially.

## Track Index

- **Composition & Songwriting** — Developing advanced melodic prosody, strict species counterpoint, classical formal architecture, and post-tonal harmonic systems.
- **Jazz Theory & Improvisation** — Mastering chord-scale theory, spontaneous linear soloing, and small-to-large jazz ensemble orchestration.
- **Music Production & Audio Engineering** — Translating musical performances into release-ready recordings through tracking physics, mixing acoustics, and mastering standards.
- **Orchestration & Arranging** — Scoring for orchestral instrument families, balancing dynamic ensemble textures, and sequencing realistic virtual MIDI mockups.
- **Electronic Music & Sound Design** — Designing synthetic timbres, programming procedural audio algorithms, and producing modern electronic compositions.

## Composition & Songwriting

Covers the technical and artistic mechanics of creating original music across contemporary popular songwriting, classical polyphony, structural form, and twentieth-century modernist languages.

Songwriting & Lyric Prosody: [Songwriting: Writing the Lyrics (Pat Pattison / Berklee, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/songwriting-lyrics) - A practical course teaching the emotional relationship between lyric structure, rhyme schemes, meter stability, and musical phrasing.

Contrapuntal Voice Leading: [Applied Counterpoint (Alan Belkin, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSntcNF64SVW2hG6S7j78_cXg_13ZWN0q) - A 24-lecture sequence that approaches species counterpoint, canon, and fugal writing as expressive compositional tools rather than historic academic drills.

Musical Form & Thematic Development: [Fundamentals of Musical Composition (Arnold Schoenberg)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780571196586) - The definitive compositional treatise on crafting motives, sentences, periods, and large-scale classical structures from minuet to sonata-allegro form.

Post-Tonal & 20th-Century Systems: [Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom (Robert Hutchinson)](https://musictheory.pugetsound.edu/mt21c/MusicTheory.html) - An open-access text providing systematic coverage of pitch-class set theory, twelve-tone serial operations, pandiatonicism, and minimalism.

## Jazz Theory & Improvisation

Explores the theoretical foundations, improvisational cognitive models, and arranging traditions that define the jazz idiom.

Jazz Harmonic Grammar: [The Jazz Theory Book (Mark Levine)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781883217044) - The canonical reference manual covering chord-scale relationships, modal interchange, tritone substitutions, and functional keyboard voicings with master transcriptions.

Melodic Improvisation: [Jazz Improvisation (Gary Burton / Berklee, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/jazz-improvisation) - A foundational guide to spontaneous linear composition, scale-degree targeting, guide tones, and the mental discipline required for cohesive solos.

Jazz Ensemble Arranging: [21M.342 Composing for Jazz Orchestra (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21m-342-composing-for-jazz-orchestra-fall-2008/) - An advanced university course surveying sectional scoring, brass and reed voicings, rhythm section notation, and score analysis from Duke Ellington to Gil Evans.

## Music Production & Audio Engineering

Examines the physical acoustics, electroacoustic transducers, signal processing techniques, and critical listening skills needed to record, mix, and deliver professional music.

Tracking & Recording Techniques: [21M.380 Music and Technology: Recording Techniques and Audio Production (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21m-380-music-and-technology-recording-techniques-and-audio-production-fall-2016/) - An academic and practical course covering transducer physics, stereo microphone configurations, preamp gain staging, and acoustic session capture.

Mixing Engineering: [Mixing Secrets for the Small Studio (Mike Senior)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781032840451) - An indispensable guide to frequency slotting, dynamic control, spatial panning, reverb placement, and problem-solving in non-treated home acoustics.

Audio Mastering: [Mastering Audio: The Art and the Science (Bob Katz)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780240818962) - The industry-standard authority on monitoring calibration, equalization, multiband dynamics, K-System metering, dither, and delivery specifications.

Production Arranging & Workflow: [The Art of Music Production (Berklee, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/producing-music) - A focused video course detailing the creative role of the producer in guiding arrangements, eliciting emotional vocal performances, and establishing sonic signatures.

## Orchestration & Arranging

Focuses on the mechanical capabilities, ranges, and timbral colors of acoustic orchestral instruments, alongside traditional scoring techniques and contemporary MIDI sequencing workflows.

Symphonic Instrumentation: [The Study of Orchestration (Samuel Adler)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780393283730) - The premier conservatory reference on the mechanics, registers, dynamic profiles, extended techniques, and idiomatic notation of every orchestral instrument.

Practical Orchestral Textures: [Applied Orchestration (Alan Belkin, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSntcNF64SVXBSwQx7kDHiF7V49lABSxC) - A 42-part video lecture series analyzing tutti scoring, textural foreground/background relationships, instrumental doubling, and tonal balance.

19th-Century Principles & Score Analysis: [Principles of Orchestration (Nikolay Rimsky-Korsakov / Garritan Interactive)](https://www.garritan.com/principles-of-orchestration/introduction) - An interactive realization of the seminal historical text containing over 270 audio-synced full-orchestra score excerpts illustrating color combinations.

Contemporary & Virtual Orchestration: [Acoustic and MIDI Orchestration for the Contemporary Composer (Andrea Pejrolo and Richard DeRosa)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781138801509) - A practical bridge between traditional acoustic arranging and modern digital production, detailing sample articulation keyswitches, CC automation, and realistic mockup design.

## Electronic Music & Sound Design

Investigates electronic sound generation, synthesis architectures, digital signal processing theory, and computer-based composition techniques.

Procedural Audio & Pure Data Programming: [21M.380 Music and Technology: Sound Design (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21m-380-music-and-technology-sound-design-spring-2016/) - A university lab course focused on analyzing and synthesizing real-world acoustic phenomena from the ground up using the open-source Pure Data dataflow language.

Comprehensive Synthesis Architecture: [Synth Secrets (Gordon Reid / Sound on Sound)](https://www.soundonsound.com/series/synth-secrets-sound-sound) - A classic 63-part pedagogical series dissecting the acoustic physics, filter networks, envelopes, and signal paths of subtractive, FM, and physical-modeling synthesizers.

DAW Production & Workflow: [Electronic Music Production Specialization (Berklee, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/electronic-music-production) - A comprehensive sequence teaching hardware/software instrument integration, MIDI composition, software synthesizers, and electronic track arrangement.

Computer Music Systems & Digital Audio: [The Computer Music Tutorial (Curtis Roads)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780262044912) - The definitive encyclopedic text on digital audio theory, granular synthesis, algorithmic composition, spatialization, and software architectures.
