## Introduction

Music as an academic discipline bridges intuitive artistic expression with a rigorous structural language. Studying music systematically goes far beyond mechanical instrument practice: it involves understanding the symbolic and acoustic principles governing pitch, rhythm, harmony, and form, while cultivating an ear capable of analyzing those relationships in real time. For an independent learner, acquiring this foundational literacy transforms passive listening into active comprehension and gives you the tools to create, interpret, and discuss music with clarity and intention.

This curriculum assumes no prior background in music theory, notation, or formal performance. It is designed for self-taught musicians, aspiring composers, producers, and active listeners who want to build the equivalent of a first-year undergraduate music foundation from the ground up. You do not need institutional access or a high-end instrument to begin, though you will need a basic keyboard—an inexpensive MIDI controller, portable digital piano, or even a virtual keyboard app—alongside a willingness to practice daily listening. Music theory cannot be internalized through reading alone; it must be connected continuously to physical execution and auditory recognition.

### Curriculum Structure

The curriculum comprises five foundational subjects modeled on the core sequence of a university music program. Rather than working through each subject in isolation, your study should balance linear prerequisites with concurrent practical disciplines:

Music Fundamentals is the strict prerequisite for the entire curriculum. You must begin here to establish fluency in reading staff notation, key signatures, meters, intervals, and triad construction before tackling more advanced material.

Once you have command of fundamentals, proceed to Diatonic Harmony & Voice Leading to study chord function, cadence patterns, and the traditional voice-leading principles that underpin tonal music.

Concurrently with your theoretical studies, make Ear Training & Aural Skills and Keyboard Proficiency daily habits from the very start. Ear training develops internal hearing (audiation) and transcription skills, ensuring that written notation remains tethered to living sound. Keyboard study serves as your visual and tactile laboratory: the piano layout offers the clearest physical map of intervals, scale degrees, and harmonic voice leading, regardless of what instrument you ultimately play.

Music History & Appreciation can be studied in parallel with harmony to provide stylistic context, active listening strategies, and historical perspective across major musical eras.

### Scope

This curriculum is deliberately focused on the universal core of music literacy and analysis. It leaves out specialized genres, career tracks, and advanced technical workflows—such as commercial songwriting, species counterpoint, jazz chord-scale theory, orchestral scoring, and digital synthesis.

After completing this core, you can branch out into specialized subdisciplines in [Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md) and build tangible creative deliverables in [Projects](projects.md).

## Curriculum

### Music Fundamentals

Covers the basic written grammar of Western music, including pitch notation across clefs, rhythmic meter, key signatures, scales, intervals, and triad construction.

[Music Theory Lessons (musictheory.net)](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons) - An interactive, self-paced sequence covering notation, intervals, scales, and chords with immediate comprehension exercises.

[Music Theory Fundamentals (Michael New, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1302D94F247600CD) - An alternative to musictheory.net that explains foundational theory concepts through visual piano demonstrations and clear whiteboard illustrations.

[Fundamentals of Music Theory (University of Edinburgh, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/edinburgh-music-theory) - A structured university MOOC that serves as an academic lecture-and-quiz alternative to the self-guided options above.

### Ear Training & Aural Skills

Develops internal pitch awareness, solfège fluency, melodic and harmonic recognition, and rhythmic precision to bridge written theory with active musical hearing.

[Developing Your Musicianship (Berklee College of Music, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/develop-your-musicianship) - A structured video course providing guided vocalization and listening exercises to internalize basic intervals, scales, and chord qualities.

[Foundations of Aural Skills (Timothy Chenette, UEN Pressbooks)](https://uen.pressbooks.pub/auralskills/) - A comprehensive open-access textbook that complements the Berklee course with systematic strategies for audiation, sight-singing, transcription, and dictation.

### Diatonic Harmony & Voice Leading

Explores functional harmonic progressions, chord inversions, cadences, and standard four-part voice leading principles within tonal Western music.

[Open Music Theory, Version 2 (Pressbooks)](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/) - The primary open-access textbook for studying chord functions, voice leading guidelines, and phrase models with embedded audio examples.

[21M.301 Harmony and Counterpoint I (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21m-301-harmony-and-counterpoint-i-spring-2005/) - A complementary course archive providing syllabus benchmarks and homework problem sets to practice four-part vocal and keyboard harmonization.

### Music History & Appreciation

Surveys the stylistic development of Western art music from antiquity and the Middle Ages through the modern era while training active analytical listening.

[MUSI 112: Listening to Music (Open Yale Courses)](https://oyc.yale.edu/music/musi-112) - A complete 23-lecture university course introducing active listening techniques, orchestral instruments, musical architecture, and major stylistic periods.

[A History of Western Music (J. Peter Burkholder, Donald Jay Grout, and Claude V. Palisca)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780393668179) - The canonical undergraduate survey text that serves as an in-depth reading companion to Wright's lectures, tracing repertoires, genres, and historical context.

### Keyboard Proficiency

Introduces functional keyboard mechanics, hand posture, grand staff reading, and five-finger patterns to enable tactile visualization of theoretical concepts.

[Hoffman Academy (Hoffman Academy)](https://www.hoffmanacademy.com) - A comprehensive free video curriculum that builds physical keyboard technique, reading fluency, and simple repertoire from the ground up.

[Alfred's Basic Adult All-in-One Piano Course, Level 1 (Alfred Music)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=0882848186) - A self-paced printed method book alternative to Hoffman Academy that is written specifically for adult beginners and integrates reading, technique, and chord theory.
